# Attribution & Upstream Suite

Magic Studio builds on community-maintained open-source libraries and interoperates with the ArtCraft creative suite:

- **[`storytold/craft-libs`](https://github.com/storytold/craft-libs)** (`MIT OR Apache-2.0`) — shared AI contract (`craft-ai-core`), document paths (`craft-docpath`), atomic file I/O (`craft-atomic`), document previews (`craft-doc`), font catalog & store (`craft-font-catalog`, `craft-font-store`, `craft-font-manager`), audio & media containers (`craft-audio`, `craft-media`), `craft-timeline`, `craft-captions`, `craft-mesh`, `craft-drawingml`, `craft-zip`, `craft-ui` (`docking`, `WidgetPalette`, `IconButton`, `SvgIconSet`, `CaptureSlot`), `craft-menu`, `craft-dialogs`, `craft-keymap`, `craft-platform`, `craft-quit`, `craft-settings`, `craft-update`, and `craft-window`.
- **[`storytold/egui`](https://github.com/storytold/egui)** & **[`storytold/winit`](https://github.com/storytold/winit)** (`MIT OR Apache-2.0`) — `egui` 0.37, `eframe` 0.37, `egui_extras` 0.37, and cross-platform windowing.
- **[`storytold/craft-common-actions`](https://github.com/storytold/craft-common-actions)** & **[`storytold/craft-contrib`](https://github.com/storytold/craft-contrib)** — CI/release workflows and ArtCraft house rules.
- **Crafting Applications** (exchanging native files on disk and driven via `<craft>-cli` / `--control`): [`DeckCraft`](https://github.com/storytold/deckcraft), [`AniCraft`](https://github.com/storytold/anicraft), [`PhotoCraft`](https://github.com/storytold/photocraft), [`VectorCraft`](https://github.com/storytold/vectorcraft), [`DesignCraft`](https://github.com/storytold/designcraft), [`EffectCraft`](https://github.com/storytold/effectcraft), and [`FilmCraft`](https://github.com/storytold/filmcraft).
